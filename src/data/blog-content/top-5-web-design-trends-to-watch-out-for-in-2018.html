Top 5 Web Design Trends to Watch Out For in 2018

Want to know which design trends are popular this year? Well then, you are at the right place since in the following sections we will be looking at some of the top design trends for 2018.

Top 5 Design Trends for 2018

Design Trend #1: Depth and Drop Shadows

Source – Clearbit.com

This year we have witnessed many surprising variations in use of shadows. Moreover, presence of parallax layouts and grids is facilitating use of shadows for creation of illusion and depth.

Web designers are more inclined towards use of shadows since it helps in creation of astonishingly versatile effects. These effects enhance user experience your website is able to provide and also augment its aesthetic appeal.

For instance, shadows are commonly used for showing hover states of a link and designers have started combining such shadows with color gradients to provide shadows three dimensional effect.

Design Trend #2: Saturated and Vibrant Color Schemes

Source – EgWineCo.com

Design teams are pouring colors liberally in websites they are creating. Some examples include use of vibrant shades and supersaturation.

This trend has been facilitated by present day monitor screens which are capable of rendering rich vibrant colors.

Design Trend #3: Particle Backgrounds

Source – DesignBetter.co

An image is worth a thousand words and designers are putting this proverb to great use in 2018.

When there is a video background, it can result in performance issues. But particle backgrounds solve this issue since the animations these backgrounds show are created using lightweight javascript.

This, in turn, facilitates creation of movements that become background’s natural part and do not take a lot of time to load.

Design Trend #4: Customized Illustrations

Source – Zingle.me

This trend is becoming popular in 2018 since illustrations can be used for creating images which are friendly and at the same time playful.

Designers have started using illustrations for adding an element of entertainment on websites. Additionally, these illustrations carry their own personality and can be tailored according to brand’s tone.

Design Trend #5: Bold Typography

Source – OursRoux.com

Designers understand that bold typography can work as a strong visual tool that can evoke emotions, develop personality, and set the right tone on a website.

Likewise, CSS-enabled hand-made typefaces consisting of large letters and contrasting headings (serif and sans serif) assist in creation of dynamic parallels, draw visitors onto reading content on your website, and provide better user experience (UX).
