WordPress: Flexible. Familiar. Widely Supported.

Why WordPress?

  • Open-source and endlessly customizable

  • Massive plugin ecosystem

  • Strong CMS capabilities for blogs and content-heavy sites

  • Supported by most hosting platforms

Great For:

  • Content-driven websites

  • Early-stage startups needing quick deployment

  • SEO-focused businesses

  • Tight budgets

But here’s the caveat:
While WordPress is powerful, it can feel bloated. Plugins can conflict. Speed suffers. Updates and security need constant attention.

As a seasoned website development company in Bangalore, India, we recommend WordPress for startups prioritizing content and speed to launch, with a plan to scale or replatform later.

Webflow: Visual-First. Design-Led. Faster to Ship.

Why Webflow?

  • Pixel-perfect control, without coding

  • Visual development = faster prototyping

  • Built-in hosting, CMS, and animations

  • Easy for non-technical founders to manage

Great For:

  • Design-led startups

  • MVPs, landing pages, and brand sites

  • Quick iteration and A/B testing

  • Teams with in-house marketing and no dev support

Webflow gives control back to creators. But it has limits—especially with dynamic logic, integrations, or scaling complex backend workflows.

As a web design company in Bangalore, IM Solutions leverages Webflow for rapid builds when agility and aesthetics are the top priority.

Custom Code: Limitless. Scalable. Engineered for Growth.

Why Custom Code?

  • Built exactly for your business logic

  • Fully optimized performance and security

  • No platform limits or plugin dependencies

  • Scales as your startup scales

Great For:

  • SaaS platforms and apps

  • High-performance websites

  • Products needing custom integrations

  • Funded startups preparing for long-term scaling

Custom development is an investment. But it’s one that pays off if your product is core to your business.
