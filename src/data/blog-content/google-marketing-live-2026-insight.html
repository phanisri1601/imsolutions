The Era of Agentic Commerce & Universal Commerce Protocol

E-commerce is entering a completely new phase.

Google calls it Agentic Commerce.

The idea is simple:
AI agents will increasingly shop on behalf of consumers.

Instead of manually searching through products, users will rely on intelligent systems to:

  • Compare
  • Curate
  • Recommend
  • Purchase

And brands must optimize for AI buyers — not just human ones.

Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)

At the center of this transformation is the Universal Commerce Protocol.

UCP standardizes commerce data at the SKU level, helping AI systems:

  • Understand products contextually
  • Recommend compatible bundles
  • Compare attributes intelligently
  • Generate conversational shopping experiences

This makes structured product data more important than ever.

AI-Assisted Checkout Experiences

Google also introduced:

  • Native Checkout integrations
  • Cross-retailer shopping journeys
  • AI-assisted purchase flows
  • Buy-now-pay-later integrations via Klarna & Affirm

Friction is disappearing.

Commerce is becoming conversational.
