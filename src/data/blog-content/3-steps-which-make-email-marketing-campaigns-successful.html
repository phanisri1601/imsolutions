Step 1: Build Email List

Your email campaign will be successful when there is a sizable list of emails. You can achieve this task by giving away things for free such as a newsletter. When asking for email from a visitor you will have to ensure that your request answers these concerns:

  1. Will I get spammed?
  2. Are you offering any discounts?
  3. What is the reward for giving the email address?
  4. At which frequency I will get the emails?
  5. Will you send me junk or relevant offers?

Step 2: Live Up to Expectations

You can expect a positive outcome from your email campaign when there is a strong CTA (call to action) and you follow up consistently. For instance, if one email is supposed to be sent on a weekly basis then do not send emails daily otherwise your campaign will fail.

Similarly, if people are expecting updates on daily basis about your products and services and you are not able to deliver then also the campaign will not succeed.

Step 3: Analysis and Segmentation

Let us first look at role of analytics in email marketing and then we will move into the topic of segmentation.

In terms of analytics you will have to check click through rate, open rate, and the rate of unsubscribes.

  1. Open rate will tell how successful you have been in building relationship with your subscribers.
  2. Lower click through rate will indicate that it is not a targeted message.
  3. If rate of unsubscribing in higher than rate of opt-in then it will imply that your email marketing company in Bangalore will have to seriously look into issues that are affecting performance of your campaign.

Segmentation is the process of dividing email list into smaller but more targeted groups. This way it becomes possible for your email marketing agency in Bangalore to create targeted campaigns.
