Why should be your Website design and SEO agency same ?

You launched a website. You want to make it run without a hitch. To do this, you have put blood and sweat into your website so that your business has the best online identity possible. Then finally after sometime, the perfectly launched website does not perform as well as you thought or had planned. You begin to wonder what went wrong and then frustration creeps in.

This is because your website lacks a balanced Search Engine Optimisation or SEO in its website content. The sooner you fix it, the better the chances it will have for it to get noticed online in the first few pages of the search engines. While it may seem like the SEO needs and website development should be outsourced by two different specialist firms for better results, the reality is different. It is a terrible idea because working with two different agencies to handle your website and SEO needs separately, will intertwined tasks leading to confusion, miscommunication, and disappointing results.

For a result-oriented performance, it is advised to work with the same website design agency to work on your SEO to ensure your website ranks to the top of the search engines and help you drive new visitors and customers each day.

Here are the top reasons why your Website design and SEO agency should be the same

1. Well planned SEO content

Using the same agency for SEO and website development will not only ensure reliability but also the effectiveness of your online visibility. The effectiveness of a site’s SEO, after all, is highly dependent on that site’s content and construction which should be pre-planned. Search engine algorithms will index your website and begin ranking it right after launch. Therefore the smart thing to do is to include SEO with website content from the start.

2. Accountability

Using one firm for both tasks promotes accountability because there is one team where members are already adept and experienced at communicating with one another. It simplifies planning and a rapid response in the event of issues that may occur during the creation of your site and the build-up to its launch. When one group is dedicated in working together, it ensures continued growth and success of your business. When you know the chaos has been avoided, it gives you a peace of mind as compared to owners trying to juggle the needs and demands of two separate firms.

3. Save time

When one team is working on the same goal, there’s no time wasted. When two firms handle separate tasks, there is a possibility of shifting blame or trying to make themselves look better than the other guy when things go wrong. It also becomes easier to regularly-scheduled meetings with just one company. Setting a time for you to meet simultaneously with two different companies is not only time consuming but also a nightmare on its own leading to frustrations and arguments on how to improve the performance of your site and its marketing.

4. Enhanced collaboration

Collaboration is very important to ensure measurable results. This can be achieved when one firm is working on the same project. When one firm is responsible for both tasks, then the members of that firm are incentivized to work together. They collaborate better to ensure that your site and its associated SEO are developed in concert to be as effective as possible. Designers are working with the SEO team, programmers with the SEO team, making a well- collaborative effort. Any suggestions for improvement of the website’s performance can also be implemented instantly than if they had to sell that suggestion to someone they barely know at an entirely different company.
