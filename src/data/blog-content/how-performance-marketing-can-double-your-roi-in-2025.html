1. What Is Performance Marketing?

Performance marketing is a results-focused advertising strategy where you only pay when a specific action is completed—like a sale, click, or form fill. It brings accountability to digital marketing and is ideal for brands that want to scale efficiently and predictably.

Core Channels Include:

  • Google Ads (Search, Display)

  • Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)

  • YouTube Video Campaigns

  • Affiliate Marketing

  • Programmatic Ads
