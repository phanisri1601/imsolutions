Market your brand to residential societies in Bangalore

In Bangalore and several other cities in India, you will see that residential colonies have a Resident Welfare Association for each colony and each block. For example, if you are living at BTM Layout, it will have an independent BTM Layout Welfare Association.

As one of the finest metropolitan cities of India, Bangalore is blessed to have some good resident welfare associations. Bangalore has more than 70, 000 registered cooperative housing societies. The residential welfare associations in Bangalore are well organised and work actively to benefit the members of the residential community. They take up several issues that affect all of the members of the residential association such as issues related to roads, sanitation, stray dogs, sanitation, waste segregation and disposal, maintenance of parks, parking spaces, etc.

Businesses should consider there are an average of 100 residents per society. It means they have the potential to reach out to more than 70, 00, 000 people in Bangalore Alone. With a closer look on the demographic details of people living in them, the chances of targeting relevant customers are even more.
